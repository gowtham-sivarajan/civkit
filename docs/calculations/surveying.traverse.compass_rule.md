# Closed traverse: misclosure and compass-rule (Bowditch) adjustment

> **Disclaimer.** CivKit results support, and do not replace, the judgement of a qualified engineer.

`surveying.traverse.compass_rule` · version 1

From the azimuth and horizontal distance of each leg of a closed traverse: the latitude (d·cos α) and departure (d·sin α) of every leg, the misclosure of the loop, the relative misclosure E/P (the precision ratio is 1 : P/E), and the compass-rule corrections, which share the misclosure out in proportion to leg length, and the adjusted latitudes and departures. A correction is the negative of the misclosure shared by length; an adjusted value is the computed value plus its correction.

## Inputs

| Name | Meaning | Dimension | Unit | Required | Domain (SI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bearings` | Azimuth of each leg, clockwise from north, in order of measurement. Quadrant bearings (unit "bearing", e.g. "N 3°45' E") or decimal angles (deg, rad, grad). Lists of DMS values are not supported. | list of angle | deg (SI: rad); or `bearing` | yes | any |
| `distances` | Horizontal distance of each leg, in the same order as the bearings | list of L | m | yes | > 0 |

**List group `legs`** (one entry per leg, at least 3): `bearings`, `distances`. Traverse legs in order of measurement. Leg k runs from station k to station k + 1; the last leg ends at station 1.

## Method

The steps of the calculation, in order, as its trace records them (worked example below):

1. `for each leg: bearings: bearing N 3d45m E → azimuth (clockwise from north)`
2. `for each leg: Lat = d·cos α`
3. `for each leg: Dep = d·sin α`
4. `P = Σd`
5. `M_N = ΣLat`
6. `M_E = ΣDep`
7. `E = √(M_N² + M_E²)`
8. `E/P`
9. `for each leg: c_N = −M_N·d/P`
10. `for each leg: c_E = −M_E·d/P`
11. `for each leg: Lat′ = Lat + c_N`
12. `for each leg: Dep′ = Dep + c_E`

## Validity and limits

- Every input is checked against its mathematical domain (table above); a value outside it is an `OUT_OF_DOMAIN` error.
- Validity range: none is stated, because no reliable source for one has been established. The mathematical domain of every input is still enforced.

## Assumptions

- The traverse is closed: the last leg ends at the first station. Open (link) traverses are not supported.
- The bearings are already balanced; angular misclosure is outside this calculation.
- Plane grid, no geodesy: distances are horizontal and measured on the grid.
- The compass rule shares the misclosure out in proportion to leg length; it does not model the sources of error.
