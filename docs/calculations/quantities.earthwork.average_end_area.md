# Earthwork volumes: average end area, with the zero-end pyramid on request

`quantities.earthwork.average_end_area` · version 1

Cut and fill volumes between consecutive cross-sections from their end areas. With `average_end_area` every segment is (A₁ + A₂)/2·L. With `pyramid_at_zero_end` a segment whose cut (or fill) area is zero at exactly one end is a pyramid, A·L/3; all other segments use the average. The method is a required input and is the first step of the trace: the engine never chooses it. Net fill is total fill minus total cut (positive: fill must be brought in).

## Inputs

| Name | Meaning | Dimension | Unit | Required | Domain (SI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `method` | How segment volumes are computed: `average_end_area` (every segment) or `pyramid_at_zero_end` (average end area, except A·L/3 where exactly one end area of a kind is zero). Required; there is no default. | — | one of: `average_end_area`, `pyramid_at_zero_end` | yes | — |
| `chainages` | Chainage of each cross-section, strictly increasing (station 0+50 is 50 m) | list of L | m | yes | any |

| `fill_areas` | Fill cross-section area at each station; 0 where there is no fill | list of L^2 | m^2 | yes | ≥ 0 |

**List group `stations`** (one entry per station, at least 2): `chainages`, `cut_areas`, `fill_areas`. Cross-section stations in order of increasing chainage. Segment k lies between station k and station k + 1.

## Method

The steps of the calculation, in order, as its trace records them (worked example below):

1. `method = average_end_area (choice)`
2. `for each segment: cut volume = (A₁ + A₂)/2·L`
3. `for each segment: fill volume = (A₁ + A₂)/2·L`
4. `ΣV_cut`
5. `ΣV_fill`
6. `net fill = ΣV_fill − ΣV_cut`

## Assumptions

- End areas are cross-sectional areas perpendicular to the centreline at the stations, and the section changes linearly between stations.
- Average end area is an approximation. It overstates a segment that tapers to a zero end (a pyramid has A·L/3, not A·L/2), which is why the pyramid method is offered, and never applied automatically.
- No shrink or swell, no curvature correction, and no prismoidal correction (not in 0.1).
- Cut and fill are not netted within a station.
