`fluids.open_channel.manning_trapezoidal` · version 1

Flow area, wetted perimeter, hydraulic radius, mean velocity and discharge of uniform flow in a trapezoidal channel at a given depth: A = (B + z·y)·y, P = B + 2y·√(1 + z²), R = A/P, V = (1/n)·R^(2/3)·S^(1/2), Q = V·A. A rectangle (z = 0) and a triangle (B = 0) are special cases.

## Method

The steps of the calculation, in order, as its trace records them (worked example below):

1. `A = (B + z·y)·y`
2. `P = B + 2·y·√(1 + z²)`
3. `R = A / P`
4. `V = (1/n)·R^(2/3)·S^(1/2)`
5. `Q = V·A`

## Assumptions

- Steady, uniform flow: the bed slope equals the energy slope.
- Prismatic trapezoidal section with equal side slopes; the depth is given, not solved for.
- SI form of Manning's equation (k = 1). n is the SI coefficient, entered as a plain number; inputs in other length units are converted to metres first, so the result does not depend on them.
