`structural.beam.simply_supported` · version 1

A prismatic beam on a pin and a roller, span L, carrying a uniformly distributed load w over the full span and a point load P at distance a from the left support A. Returns both support reactions, the deflection at a requested distance x from A, and the slope at A. Loads act downward; deflection and slope are measured upward and counter-clockwise, so a downward load gives negative values, as in the cited sources. Maximum shear and moment are not part of this version: they are deferred from 0.1 while an openly licensed worked example is sought (D-20, D-25).

## Inputs

| Name | Meaning | Dimension | Unit | Required | Domain (SI) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `span` | Span L between the supports | L | m | yes | > 0 |
| `udl` | Uniformly distributed load w over the full span, acting downward | M*T^-2 | kN/m (SI: N/m) | no (default 0) | ≥ 0 |
| `point_load` | Point load P, acting downward | L*M*T^-2 | kN (SI: N) | no (default 0) | ≥ 0 |
| `point_load_position` | Distance a of the point load from support A, 0 ≤ a ≤ L; required when point_load > 0 | L | m | **conditional: required when point_load > 0** | ≥ 0 |
| `elastic_modulus` | Modulus of elasticity E | L^-1*M*T^-2 | GPa (SI: Pa) | yes | > 0 |
| `second_moment` | Second moment of area I about the bending axis | L^4 | mm^4 (SI: m^4) | yes | > 0 |
| `position` | Distance x from support A at which the deflection is wanted, 0 ≤ x ≤ L | L | m | yes | ≥ 0 |

> **Conditional requirement:** `point_load_position` is required when `point_load > 0`.

## Outputs

| Name | Meaning | Dimension | Unit | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `reaction_a` | Vertical reaction at A (left), upward positive | L*M*T^-2 | kN (SI: N) | scalar |
| `reaction_b` | Vertical reaction at B (right), upward positive | L*M*T^-2 | kN (SI: N) | scalar |
| `deflection` | Deflection at x, upward positive (downward deflection is negative) | L | mm (SI: m) | scalar |
| `end_slope_a` | Slope θ_A of the deflected shape at A, counter-clockwise positive | angle | rad | scalar |

## Method

The steps of the calculation, in order, as its trace records them (worked example below):

1. `R_A = w·L/2 + P·(L − a)/L`
2. `R_B = w·L/2 + P·a/L`
3. `EI = E·I`
4. `y_w = −w·x·(L³ − 2L·x² + x³) / (24·EI)`
5. `y_P = −P·b·x·(L² − b² − x²) / (6·L·EI), x ≤ a, b = L − a`
6. `y = y_w + y_P`
7. `θ_A = −w·L³/(24·EI) − P·b·(L² − b²)/(6·L·EI)`

## Validity and limits

- Every input is checked against its mathematical domain (table above); a value outside it is an `OUT_OF_DOMAIN` error.
- Validity range: none is stated, because no reliable source for one has been established. The mathematical domain of every input is still enforced.

## Assumptions

- Euler–Bernoulli beam theory: small deflections, plane sections remain plane, shear deformation ignored.
- Linear elastic, prismatic beam: E and I are constant along the span; self-weight is included only if it is part of w.
- Simple supports (pin at A, roller at B) at the beam ends; loads act vertically downward.
- The UDL and the point load are combined by superposition.
